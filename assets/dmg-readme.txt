FTL Hyperspace Installer
========================

INSTALLING
----------
1. Open the installer FTL-Hyperspace.app
2. macOS will say Apple "could not verify" it. Click "Done"
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security
4. Scroll down to the "Security" section and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the message about FTL-Hyperspace.app
5. Follow the on-screen steps


--------------------------
Mod manager folder:  ~/Games/FTLHyperspace
Mods folder:         ~/Games/FTLHyperspace/mods

To open it in Finder, press Cmd + Shift + G,
paste ~/Games/FTLHyperspace and press Enter.

To add more mods, drop .ftl or .zip files into the mods folder, then
open "FTL Mod Manager.app", tick the mods you want and click Apply.
